How to use ProjectRiches

Each 15-minute Kalshi window has a strike. UP wins if BTC finishes above it, DOWN if below.

The Call reads WARMING UP, then STANDBY while the model builds its read, then LOCKED around 8:00 into the window. Once locked, direction and confidence are frozen; only the countdown moves. An Early Read at 3:00 quietly feeds Hold Watch.

Cushion compares how far price is past the strike with how far BTC typically still moves in the time left. Fair value compares the model's probability with Kalshi's ask (backend mode). Smart skip checks your own graded history for similar setups.

Tabs: Patterns (candle shapes), Market (exchange prices), Pressure (order flow), Tools (RSI, MACD, strike z-score, path model), Record (results by weekday). A SKIP means the round is too close to call; sitting out is part of the edge.
